The initiative

Turn an existing AI-assisted legal analysis service into a complete digital product that customers could purchase, track, and receive through a secure, expert-reviewed workflow.

01 · Initiative and product gap

The goal was to productize an AI-assisted advisory service from order through approved delivery.

The exercise started with valuable pieces already in place. My work focused on the connected product and operating model required to create a dependable paid service.

Starting point

Service expertise and AI foundations

The advisory firm had domain expertise, account and case foundations, and AI that could read a 40–50 page Presentence Report, extract case data, and draft a security designation.

What I was trying to accomplish

A complete digital service

Give customers one secure place to understand the offer, create a case, submit the PSR, pay, follow progress, respond to questions, and receive the expert-approved report.

The product gap

The steps were still disconnected

The concept needed document validation, checkout, AI processing, expert verification, placement analysis, exception handling, approval, secure delivery, and case history to work as one system.

Product question
How can the product preserve AI speed while making expert judgment, evidence, exceptions, and approval visible?
02 · End-to-end execution flow

The initiative succeeds only when the full paid service loop works.

I mapped the decision points and recovery paths that connect a customer order to a defensible, expert-approved report.

01

Check

File usable?

02

Review and pay

Confirm scope, permissions, and the per-case order.

Check

Payment approved?

If no, preserve the case and provide a retry path.

03

AI draft and checks

Extract fields, calculate the draft designation, and flag uncertainty.

04

Expert review

Verify evidence, resolve flags, and complete placement analysis.

Check

More information?

Ask a targeted question, then return the response to review.

05

Approve and release

Record approval, lock the version, and deliver it securely.

Design targetUse AI to produce a rapid first draft. Release the customer report only after expert analysis and recorded approval.
03 · Users and responsibilities

I separated who buys, who the case is about, and who owns the release decision.

These working proto-personas came from the service and exercise constraints. They define workflow responsibilities and remain hypotheses to validate.

Primary buyer hypothesis

Legal team

Submits for a client, manages payment and access, and needs a timely, defensible result for client strategy.

Secondary customer path

Individual defendant

May submit for themselves and needs clear guidance, privacy, and appropriate sharing controls.

Expert analyst and approver

Domain expert

Verifies source evidence, resolves ambiguity, adds placement analysis, and approves the report for release.

05 · AI and expert operating model

The expert workspace is a core product experience.

Unresolved critical fields, conflicts, missing information, or incomplete placement analysis block release. The system preserves evidence and decision history.

Platform and AI

Extract and draft

Read the PSR, extract fields, calculate the draft designation, run checks, and route flags to review.

Domain expert

Verify and analyze

Compare critical fields with source pages, record overrides, request information, and add placement analysis.

Release gate

Approve and lock

Confirm each consequential item is resolved, record the approver and model version, then release securely.

07 · Proposed MVP decisions

I designed the MVP as a complete paid service loop.

The first release covers discovery through secure delivery and includes controls for high-stakes, sensitive information.

01

Use one customer flow

Support legal-team and self-submission branches through case ownership and permissions.

02

Validate before payment

Confirm the PSR is readable and processable before charging the customer.

03

Require expert approval

Record approval on every customer-visible report and block unresolved critical issues.

04

Keep delivery secure

Use plain status language and email alerts while keeping report content inside the authenticated portal.

05

Preserve the record

Retain evidence links, overrides, receipts, model and report versions, and activity history.

08 · Proposed roadmap

Launch the closed loop, improve operations, then expand responsibly.

The sequencing protects the service promise while the team learns where customers, experts, and AI need the most support.

Launch

Complete the service loop

  • Customer intake and checkout
  • AI draft and expert review
  • Status, recovery, and secure delivery
  • Audit history and permissions
Optimize

Improve quality and throughput

  • Refine validation and confidence flags
  • Reduce expert review time
  • Improve customer questions and recovery
  • Strengthen operational reporting
Scale

Expand with evidence

  • Segment workflows by case complexity
  • Introduce reusable customer accounts
  • Evaluate additional analysis services
  • Automate low-risk operational steps
09 · Measurement, assumptions, and validation

The plan measures customer completion, operational performance, and report quality together.

The targets would be set after reviewing current service baselines. The proposed measures show what the team needs to learn before scaling.

Adoption and operations
  • Case-start to paid-order conversion
  • Upload validation failure and recovery
  • Time from payment to AI draft
  • Expert review time and queue age
  • Time from approval to report access
Trust and AI quality
  • Critical-field extraction accuracy
  • Expert override rate and reason
  • Cases requiring more information
  • Reports blocked by unresolved issues
  • Customer-visible reports with recorded approval
Assumptions to test
  • Legal teams are the primary buyer
  • Submitted PSRs contain enough information
  • Experts have capacity for the review load
  • Customers understand the status model
  • Permissions and retention rules support the flow
Key risks and responses
  • AI error: evidence links, checks, and expert approval
  • Review bottleneck: queue visibility and complexity tracking
  • Missing information: targeted questions and a recovery loop
  • Sensitive data: authenticated access, permissions, and audit history
Customer evidence

Review current order data and interview both customer types to validate who buys and how cases are managed.

Operational evidence

Shadow current cases, inspect existing deliverables, and time representative PSRs by complexity.

Usability and governance

Test customer and expert flows and confirm authorization, sharing, retention, and security requirements.

MVP quality guardrail Expert approval is recorded on 100% of customer-visible reports. Unresolved critical issues block release.

The takeaway

The initiative required a product and operating model around the AI.

I connected the customer purchase, expert operations, AI quality, exceptions, security, and measurement into one proposed service workflow. AI creates speed at the start of the analysis. Domain experts retain responsibility for interpretation, placement analysis, and release.
