01 · Opportunity definition

Customers needed a structured way to manage on-the-job training inside a regulated qualification workflow.

The opportunity required more than adding a set of fields. I needed to understand the shared workflow, where customer practices differed, what belonged in the core product, and whether the value supported a separate subscription.

Customer need

Make training work easier to define, coordinate, and verify

Discovery focused on the people, decisions, evidence, and handoffs required to move training work through completion.

Product opportunity

Create one reusable product model

The core experience needed to support repeatable customer outcomes while allowing appropriate configuration for different operating environments.

Business question

Confirm that the problem justified a net-new subscription

The case had to connect customer evidence with platform fit, implementation scope, commercial value, and a credible path to launch.

02 · Customer discovery

Six customers helped separate a shared product need from customer-specific process choices.

I used advisory discussions, targeted questions, workflow analysis, and follow-up validation to compare where customers agreed and where the product would need configuration.

03 · Business case and decision

I connected customer evidence to a product and investment decision.

The business case gave leaders a clear view of the problem, product approach, commercial model, delivery implications, risks, and proposed MVP.

Customer evidence

Is the problem recurring and important?

Six-customer validation established the common need and where variation mattered.

Product fit

Does it extend the platform coherently?

The concept connected training work with the platform’s existing qualification and compliance context.

Business value

Can it support a separate subscription?

The proposal defined a net-new product with a distinct customer outcome and commercial path.

Delivery confidence

Can the team build a complete first release?

MVP boundaries, dependencies, business rules, and readiness work made the investment discussable.

04 · MVP definition

The MVP covered the smallest complete training loop.

I used the customer evidence to define the responsibilities and system behavior required for a usable first subscription, while keeping lower-value expansion outside the initial scope.

1

Define

Set up the training structure, expectations, and accountable roles.

2

Coordinate

Connect the right people with the required training work.

3

Complete

Support the field workflow and capture what happened.

4

Verify

Record completion evidence and the appropriate review.

5

Monitor

Give administrators useful status and follow-up visibility.

Included in the first product definition
  • Core roles and responsibilities
  • Configurable workflow rules
  • Training status and completion evidence
  • Administrative visibility
  • Connection to the broader qualification workflow
Protected for later learning
  • Broader workflow variants
  • Advanced reporting and optimization
  • Additional automation opportunities
  • Expansion based on adoption evidence
  • Enhancements outside the core customer outcome
